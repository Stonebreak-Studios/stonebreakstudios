AfterCure · Online co-op survival

Somewhere in this city is the cure.

Everything else in it wants you dead.

Apply for the beta Join the Stonebreak Studios Discord

Just get back before dark.

The infection took the city in weeks. The labs went dark, the streets went quiet, and the people who were racing to stop it vanished with their work half done.

You wake up on cold pavement with nothing: no supplies, no answers, only the rumour that someone got close to a cure before the end. Team up with friends, scavenge what the city left behind and push deeper every day.

What you do

A city left to rot

Overgrown streets, stalled cars and silent towers, built in Unreal Engine 5.

Survive together

Host a game or join a friend's session and play in co-op.

Scavenge everything

Search buildings for resources and loot, and manage what you carry.

Move like you mean it

Vault fences and climb over obstacles to get where you need to go.

Make your survivor

Choose a face, hair, colours and clothes the first time you spawn.

Find the cure

Somewhere in the ruins are the answers the scientists never finished finding.

The setting

They called it the Fall.

City notice · Quarantine zoneStatus: lost

The Fall was the weeks when the infection spread faster than anyone could treat it. Now vines climb the towers, cars rust where they stopped, and the infected own the night.

Do not travel after dark. Shelter before sundown.

Closed beta · Early 2027

Help us break it before launch.

AfterCure's closed beta starts in early 2027. We're looking for players who'll play hard, find what's broken and tell us what's fun and what isn't.

Apply on Discord

How to apply

Three steps

  1. Join the Stonebreak Studios Discord.

  2. Find the playtester application and fill it in. It takes a few minutes.

  3. We review every application and message you on Discord if you're in.

What testers get

  • Early access to AfterCure builds before anyone else
  • A direct line to the team in private Discord channels
  • A say in what we change

What we ask

  • Play the builds we send and report bugs in Discord
  • Keep beta builds and anything you see in them private until we say otherwise
  • A PC that meets the minimum specs, which we'll post before the beta

About Stonebreak

A small studio building big, lonely worlds.

Stonebreak Studios is an independent game studio. We make multiplayer games about surviving together: places that feel empty until your friends show up.

How we work

Break the ordinary.

We're a small team, and everyone works on the game directly. One of us builds the world and the levels; another builds the systems you play with, like inventory and gameplay. Playtesters are part of the team too. We test early, listen in Discord and change things based on what you tell us.

What we care about

Worlds worth exploring

Every street and building should feel like it had a life before you got there.

Playing together

Our games are built for co-op first.

Building in the open

We share progress and bring players in before launch, not after.

Contact

Say hello.

The fastest way to reach us is the Stonebreak Studios Discord. For press, business or anything else, email either of us directly.
